# Self-Test — ROLLUP, CUBE, GROUPING SETS

**Q1. What does ROLLUP do?**

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
It creates hierarchical subtotal rows such as totals by group and a grand total.
</details>

**Q2. What does CUBE do?**

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
It produces totals across all combinations of grouped dimensions.
</details>

**Q3. What is the main benefit of GROUPING SETS?**

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
It lets you specify exactly which grouping combinations you want.
</details>

**Q4. When would ROLLUP be useful?**

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
When you need totals by department and a final overall total.
</details>

**Q5. Which operator is better when you want all combinations of dimensions?**

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
CUBE.
</details>

**Q6. Why are these functions useful in reporting?**

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
They produce structured summary output without writing many separate queries.
</details>

---

## Quick revision

- ROLLUP = hierarchical rollup totals
- CUBE = all combination totals
- GROUPING SETS = custom mixes of groups
