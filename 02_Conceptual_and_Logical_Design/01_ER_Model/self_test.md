# Self-Test — ER Model

> Status: ✅ Ready for practice.

**Q1. What is an entity in an ER model?**

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
An entity is a real-world object or concept that we store data about, such as Student, Course, or Department.
</details>

**Q2. What is an attribute?**

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
An attribute is a property of an entity, such as StudentName, Age, or Email.
</details>

**Q3. What is a relationship?**

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
A relationship describes how two or more entities are associated, such as Student enrolls in Course.
</details>

**Q4. What is the difference between a strong entity and a weak entity?**

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
A strong entity has its own key. A weak entity depends on a parent entity for identity and usually has a partial key.
</details>

**Q5. What are cardinality constraints?**

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
They describe how many instances of one entity can be related to another, such as one-to-many or many-to-many.
</details>

**Q6. Why do we use ER diagrams?**

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
They help visualize entities, attributes, and relationships before building the relational database.
</details>

---

## Quick revision

- Entity = thing of interest
- Attribute = property
- Relationship = connection between entities
- Cardinality = how many instances participate
- ER diagram = blueprint for the database design
