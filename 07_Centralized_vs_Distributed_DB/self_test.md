# Self-Test — Centralized vs Distributed Databases

> Status: ✅ Ready for practice.

**Q1. What is a centralized database?**

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
A centralized database stores its data in one main location or server.
</details>

**Q2. What is a distributed database?**

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
A distributed database stores data across multiple sites or nodes.
</details>

**Q3. What is a major benefit of a centralized database?**

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
It is simpler to manage and has a clearer consistency model.
</details>

**Q4. What is a major benefit of a distributed database?**

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
It is typically more scalable and fault-tolerant.
</details>

**Q5. Why are distributed systems harder to manage?**

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
They require replication, synchronization, and coordination across multiple nodes.
</details>

**Q6. Which architecture is usually simpler for small applications?**

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>
A centralized architecture.
</details>

---

## Quick revision

- Centralized = simpler, single location
- Distributed = scalable, multi-location, more complex
